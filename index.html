Concept project

Crown & BladeBarbershop website concept

A premium, mobile-first website for a fictional barbershop, designed and built by Veyra Digital to show how a local business can present its services, prices and booking enquiries with real presence. All business details on the demo are placeholders.

Type
Concept project
Sector
Barbershop
Scope
Design & build
Stack
React · Vite · Tailwind
Explore the case study View live demo (opens in a new tab)

  1. Visual direction

    Black, charcoal and ivory with a muted gold accent. Bodoni Moda gives the display type a classic, barbershop-sign confidence; an animated barber pole carries the theme without stock photography.

  2. Responsive layout

    Designed mobile-first. On phones the headline stacks, actions go full-width and the menu becomes a full-screen panel; on desktop the barber pole stands alongside the headline.

  3. Services & prices

    Laid out like a printed price list — grouped into haircuts, shaves, beard and packages, with dotted leaders, durations and a note that prices are sample figures.

  4. Gallery & contact

    An editorial gallery grid with illustrated tiles, clearly labelled as placeholders for the shop’s own photography, followed by address, directions, phone, email and opening hours.

  5. Booking enquiry links

    WhatsApp links open a pre-filled appointment message. The site states plainly that it does not take live bookings — appointments are confirmed when the shop replies.
